Single-tier lockers are full-height (typically 60-72 inches) and provide maximum storage per person. Double-tier lockers stack two compartments vertically, doubling capacity in the same floor space but with smaller individual compartments. Double-tier is ideal for facilities with many employees who only need to store small items.
