Rack Protection FAQ

Steel guards provide maximum protection and are best for heavy forklift traffic and larger equipment. Polymer (plastic) guards are lighter, won't rust, and work well for lighter impacts from pallet jacks and smaller equipment. Polymer guards also won't damage forklift bodies on impact. Many facilities use steel for high-traffic areas and polymer elsewhere.

Even minor impacts can significantly reduce a rack column's load capacity. A 1-inch deflection in a standard upright can reduce capacity by 30-50%. Visible dents and bends are structural damage that requires professional assessment. Severely damaged uprights should be unloaded immediately until repaired or replaced.

Prioritize end-of-aisle locations where forklifts turn, columns at intersections, uprights near dock doors, and any columns that show existing damage or scrape marks. These high-traffic areas see the most impacts. Column guards on ground-level columns are essential since that's where forklifts strike.
