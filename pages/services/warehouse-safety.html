Warehouse Safety FAQ

OSHA requires clearly marked aisles and passageways, proper signage for hazards, separation of pedestrian and vehicle traffic in high-traffic areas, and access to PPE where required. Specific requirements vary by industry and facility type. We can help you assess your compliance needs.

Rack guards wrap around column bases and absorb forklift impacts before they damage the structural steel. A bent or dented rack column can compromise the entire system's load capacity. Guards are designed to be sacrificial - they take the damage so your racking doesn't.

Common conventions include yellow for traffic aisles and caution areas, white for equipment and production areas, green for safety and first aid, red for fire equipment and danger, and orange for inspection areas. Consistency throughout your facility is more important than specific colors.
