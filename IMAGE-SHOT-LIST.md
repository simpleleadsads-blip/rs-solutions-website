## Image Specifications

### Technical Requirements
- **Format:** JPG for photos, PNG for graphics with transparency
- **Resolution:** Minimum 1920x1080 for heroes, 1200x800 for content
- **File size:** Optimize to under 500KB for web (use compression)
- **Aspect ratios:**
  - Heroes: 16:9 or 21:9 (wide)
  - Content: 4:3 or 3:2
  - Square: 1:1 for cards/thumbnails

### Style Guidelines
- Professional, clean warehouse settings
- Good lighting (avoid dark/shadowy images)
- Show scale (include people for reference when possible)
- RS Solutions branding visible where natural
- Safety gear visible on workers (hard hats, vests)
- Clean, organized facilities (no clutter)
- Modern equipment and facilities

### Alt Text Reminders
Each image needs descriptive alt text for accessibility:
- Describe what's in the image
- Include relevant keywords naturally
- Keep under 125 characters
